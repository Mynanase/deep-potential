# Deep Potential and Flow-Based CBE Inference for Gravitational Potential Recovery from 6D Stellar Phase Space

This domain studies recovery of a galaxy’s gravitational potential directly from 6D stellar phase-space snapshots by learning a smooth distribution function or conditional velocity law and constraining it with the collisionless Boltzmann equation (CBE). The foundation-paper anchor is arXiv:2310.00040, which established the Deep Potential line in a rotating-frame setting; the best practical reference is arXiv:2507.03742, which is the concise methodology entry point for modern JAX/flow-based implementations, diagnostics, selection treatment, and stability issues. For the user’s goal, the relevant field core is static or nearly static potential recovery from mock or Gaia-like data, with emphasis on normalizing flows, exact/low-noise derivative estimation, selection effects, uncertainty quantification, non-stationarity diagnostics, and truth-level validation against simulations.

- Best reference paper: arXiv:2507.03742: Deep Potential: Recovering the gravitational potential and local pattern speed in the solar neighborhood with GDR3 using normalizing flows

## Methodology

- Model the stellar phase-space distribution with a differentiable density model, then use CBE residuals to infer the gravitational potential (or acceleration field) without imposing a rigid analytic DF.
  Papers: arXiv:2011.04673, arXiv:2205.01129, arXiv:2305.13358, arXiv:2310.00040, arXiv:2507.03742
- Normalizing flows are the dominant practical DF model because they provide smooth densities and derivatives needed for CBE-based inversion; derivative quality is a major scientific bottleneck.
  Papers: arXiv:1705.07057, arXiv:1810.01367, arXiv:2205.01129, arXiv:2305.13358, arXiv:2507.03742
- Deep Potential specializes the broader flow-plus-CBE program by jointly learning a neural potential and, when needed, a rotating-frame parameter or related nuisance structure.
  Papers: arXiv:2310.00040, arXiv:2507.03742
- Selection effects can be handled either by explicitly learning a spatial completeness/dust model together with the DF and potential, or by reformulating the problem in terms of conditional velocity distributions that are invariant to purely spatial selection.
  Papers: arXiv:2412.14236, inspire:3089973, arXiv:2512.09989, arXiv:2512.02115
- Non-stationarity is not a side issue but a first-order identifiability risk; it should be diagnosed using asymmetries, phase-space structure, independent acceleration probes, or mock truth tests before interpreting recovered density fluctuations as physical.
  Papers: arXiv:2112.07657, arXiv:2208.03667, arXiv:2310.10225, arXiv:2306.13137, arXiv:2401.15808, arXiv:2507.03742, arXiv:2512.09989
- Independent external constraints such as pulsar accelerations, circular-velocity curves, or analytic Galactic potentials are best used as validation/comparison layers, not as replacements for the data-driven local inference machinery.
  Papers: arXiv:1608.00971, arXiv:2212.10393, arXiv:2303.12838, arXiv:2306.13137, arXiv:2401.15808
- Recent alternatives widen the method space: direct score estimation can reduce derivative noise, while PINN or residual-potential formulations can improve calibration and interpretability if tied to the same truth-level validation standard.
  Papers: inspire:3089976, arXiv:2606.18386

## Known Solved Cases

Use these solved cases as examples of strong research form, not as new ideas. Do not propose a solved case itself as the core deliverable unless the proposal adds a genuinely new scientific component with substantial impact.

- Static-equilibrium recovery of local acceleration and density from mock stellar phase-space samples using learned DF derivatives plus the CBE.
  Why solved: Multiple papers demonstrate accurate recovery on controlled stationary mocks, including percent-level acceleration recovery and tractable end-to-end validation.
  Transferable form: Concrete template: choose a controlled equilibrium mock, learn a differentiable DF, compute CBE residuals, recover accelerations/density, and validate against simulator truth with spatial error maps and regime-of-validity cuts.
  Forbidden reuse: Invalid to re-propose the same central calculation: fit a normalizing flow to a stationary mock 6D tracer DF and invert the CBE to recover static accelerations/density with only minor architectural or hyperparameter changes.
  Valid new axes: new observable beyond acceleration/density maps, new regime such as sparse halo tracers or strongly anisotropic selection, new theorem/identifiability result for local recovery, substantially improved derivative-estimation method, new uncertainty-calibration framework against truth
  Papers: arXiv:2011.04673, arXiv:2205.01129, arXiv:2112.07657
- Joint recovery of gravitational potential and a rotating-frame parameter from a barred-galaxy simulation using Deep Potential.
  Why solved: The paper already demonstrates the rotating-frame extension, pattern-speed fit, and truth-level validation on a barred simulation.
  Transferable form: Strong idea template: introduce one extra physical degree of freedom, test it first in a simulation where truth is known, and validate both field recovery and failure modes under non-stationarity.
  Forbidden reuse: Invalid to repackage rotating-frame Deep Potential on barred-galaxy snapshots as a new idea unless the scientific target changes substantially away from pattern speed and beyond the original recovery/validation structure.
  Valid new axes: static-limit diagnostics that suppress spurious rotating-frame structure, new non-stationarity detectors rather than new pattern-speed fits, new mock family such as Auriga halo with realistic substructure, new uncertainty decomposition separating frame mismatch from DF error
  Papers: arXiv:2310.00040
- Large-sample local application of Deep Potential with neural selection/completeness treatment, neural potential, and flow-based DF learning in Gaia-like data.
  Why solved: This is already the modern practical implementation reference and covers the core workflow the downstream agent should not merely repeat.
  Transferable form: Use as an execution template for data preparation, training stability, completeness modeling, and interpretation of spatial fluctuations; not as a source of new central science questions by itself.
  Forbidden reuse: Invalid to simply rerun local Milky Way Deep Potential with a selection model or similar tracer set and claim novelty from implementation updates alone.
  Valid new axes: mock-to-real transfer diagnostics, static-potential recovery in a halo-dominated tracer population, exact-score replacement for noisy flow derivatives, simulation-backed uncertainty calibration tied to truth metrics
  Papers: arXiv:2507.03742
- Joint inference of unbiased phase-space density, dust/completeness effects, and gravitational potential in Gaia-like settings.
  Why solved: Selection-function and dust-correction variants are already established methodological branches with explicit validation targets.
  Transferable form: Example of a strong extension: isolate a concrete observational pathology, build a differentiable correction mechanism, and validate it against an external map or independent truth source before interpreting the recovered potential.
  Forbidden reuse: Invalid to propose dust/selection correction by learning the full observed phase-space density plus a neural spatial selection function as the main novelty if the deliverable is just another Deep Potential run.
  Valid new axes: selection-robust halo-tracer recovery in mocks, comparison between explicit-selection and conditional-velocity formulations, new diagnostics for when learned selection absorbs disequilibrium, uncertainty propagation from selection mismatch into potential error
  Papers: arXiv:2412.14236, inspire:3089973, arXiv:2512.09989
- Direct score-estimation / score-matching formulation to bypass explicit normalizing-flow density derivatives in CBE-based inference.
  Why solved: The paper already defines direct score estimation as a candidate alternative to standard flow-derivative pipelines.
  Transferable form: Methodological template: replace the fragile derivative-estimation block with a lower-noise estimator, then compare accuracy, compute cost, and scientific bias on controlled simulations.
  Forbidden reuse: Invalid to claim novelty by merely switching from density evaluation to direct score estimation without a new scientific payoff or rigorous truth-level advantage in the target regime.
  Valid new axes: exact-score estimators specialized to Deep Potential training, hybrid flow-plus-score objectives, error-budget analysis linking score noise to potential bias, implementation path that materially changes tractability in the current JAX codebase
  Papers: inspire:3089976
- Conditional Deep Potential that models p(v|x) and tracer density to avoid explicit spatial selection-function modeling.
  Why solved: The reformulation and mock validation are already done; reusing that central move alone is not new.
  Transferable form: Excellent strong-idea form: remove a brittle nuisance model by reformulating the learned object, prove or demonstrate what invariances are gained, and test on mocks with structured dust/selection.
  Forbidden reuse: Invalid to present 'ignore spatial selection by modeling p(v|x)' as a new standalone idea without a new regime, theorem, or validation gain relevant to the user’s problem.
  Valid new axes: apply conditional formulation to halo snapshots with strong radial incompleteness, compare bias/variance against explicit-selection Deep Potential in Auriga-like mocks, derive conditions where conditional recovery is identifiable or fails, combine conditional formulation with uncertainty-calibrated truth tests
  Papers: arXiv:2512.02115

## Open Axes for New Work

These axes are examples, not a complete list. Use them to look for substantial differences from solved work, and actively discover additional axes from the user prompt, source papers, and novelty checks.

- Auriga-halo mock validation for static-potential recovery in a realistically imperfect but truth-known setting
  Guidance: This is directly aligned with the user’s target. Keep the first calculation concrete: one controlled Auriga snapshot, one static target potential, one truth metric suite. New work should come from the validation design, failure analysis, or new observable/regime, not from re-running baseline Deep Potential unchanged.
  Example variations: compare tracer selections by radius, metallicity proxy, or bound/unbound substructure masking, test local vs multi-patch recovery instead of a single solar-neighborhood box, evaluate where halo disequilibrium breaks static inference first
  Papers: arXiv:2310.00040, arXiv:2507.03742, arXiv:2405.19410
- Derivative-estimation upgrades inside Deep Potential
  Guidance: Promising if it materially changes training stability or potential bias in the current JAX repository. A valid project needs a scientific consequence, not just faster training.
  Example variations: replace flow-derived scores with direct score estimation, hybrid exact-score plus likelihood objective, quantify bias/variance of first- and second-derivative estimators on the same mock
  Papers: inspire:3089976, arXiv:2205.01129, arXiv:2305.13358, arXiv:2507.03742
- Selection-robust formulations tuned to mock survey cuts
  Guidance: Useful because the user explicitly wants reliable recovery under realistic data processing constraints. Novelty should come from a new regime comparison, failure theorem, or uncertainty effect, not from merely adopting an existing formulation.
  Example variations: explicit neural selection model vs conditional p(v|x) formulation on identical Auriga-derived observations, radially varying incompleteness or dust-like masks in mock halo samples, stress tests where selection correlates with disequilibrium tracers
  Papers: arXiv:2412.14236, arXiv:2512.02115, arXiv:2512.09989
- Non-stationarity diagnostics and veto criteria for static inference
  Guidance: The deliverable should be a decision rule or measurable diagnostic linked to truth-level potential error. Pattern speed should remain secondary.
  Example variations: train a diagnostic head or residual test that flags regions where CBE-consistent static recovery is unreliable, compare recovered potential bias against phase-snail, north/south asymmetry, or independent-acceleration indicators, define acceptance criteria for when static Deep Potential can be trusted on halo mocks
  Papers: arXiv:2112.07657, arXiv:2208.03667, arXiv:2310.10225, arXiv:2401.15808, arXiv:2507.03742, arXiv:2512.09989
- Uncertainty quantification calibrated against simulation truth
  Guidance: A strong contribution would tie uncertainty estimates to actual coverage of force/potential errors in Auriga mocks. Mere error bars without calibration are weak.
  Example variations: bootstrap over tracer resampling and selection realizations, ensemble or Bayesian neural potential variants, separate epistemic, finite-sample, and disequilibrium-induced uncertainty components
  Papers: arXiv:2507.03742, arXiv:2512.09989, arXiv:2606.18386
- Residual or structured-prior potential models rather than fully free neural potentials
  Guidance: This is attractive if the current repo struggles with stability or extrapolation. It must be justified by improved scientific behavior on truth tests, not just nicer plots.
  Example variations: analytic Milky-Way-like prior plus neural residual, coordinate/radial scaling tailored to halo recovery, compare interpretability and extrapolation against fully free potentials
  Papers: arXiv:1608.00971, arXiv:2606.18386
- Cross-validation against independent gravitational observables
  Guidance: This is an example axis, not a complete list. It is strongest when used to falsify artifacts or rank model variants, not to replace the main phase-space inference.
  Example variations: compare local recovered accelerations with pulsar-based LOS acceleration templates, compare radial force profiles with circular-velocity-curve constraints, use external probes only as post-fit validation layers
  Papers: arXiv:2306.13137, arXiv:2401.15808, arXiv:2212.10393, arXiv:2303.12838
